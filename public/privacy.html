Plantation Sector · MJM Portal · Privacy Policy · Last updated 2 October 2026

Who we are

The MJM Portal is an internal system used by MJM (Plantation Sector) to manage machinery and maintenance requests. It is not a public service.

What we collect

When an authorised staff member or contractor sends a WhatsApp message to our business number, we store:

  • the sender's WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
  • the message text, or the photo and its caption;
  • the date and time the message was sent and technical message IDs from WhatsApp.

Messages from numbers that are not on our authorised list are logged only and are not processed.

Why we use it

Only to receive, review and follow up machinery maintenance requests, and to send an automatic reply confirming that a message or photo was received. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a private database and private file storage (Supabase). Only signed-in MJM portal administrators can view it. Messages are delivered to us through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta.

How long we keep it

For as long as it is needed for maintenance records of our machinery, after which it is deleted.

Deleting your data

To have your messages and photos deleted, contact the MJM HQ office or the MJM portal administrator and give the WhatsApp number you sent from. We will delete the related records and confirm when it is done.
